---
name: Contract dispute handling
description: Product boundary between contract drafting and the standalone arbitration service.
---

Contract drafting must offer a compact “آلية النزاع” choice between judiciary and arbitration, after the amicable-settlement period. Judiciary is the default. Arbitration-center and arbitrator-count controls remain hidden until the user explicitly selects arbitration.

**Why:** The user clarified that the mistake was making arbitration occupy excessive space and look like a separate service—not offering it as a contract clause. The two dispute-resolution choices must stay available in a compact form.

**How to apply:** Keep the control inline and small in the drafting settings. Preserve the selected method in server-side drafting instructions; never default it to arbitration or show detailed arbitration fields before the user selects that option.
